Objective:
Assess this regional hospital network's organizational, data, and workforce readiness for AI adoption across up to three candidate use cases (e.g., ambient clinical documentation, radiology triage support, revenue-cycle automation), and deliver a prioritized roadmap the network can fund and staff. This is a readiness assessment and roadmap, not a build — it does not commit to any tool selection, model deployment, or measured outcome improvement.

Body:
**In scope:** (1) Current-state assessment of data infrastructure, EHR integration points, and governance maturity for the 3 candidate use cases; (2) stakeholder interviews (target: 20–25 across clinical, IT, compliance, revenue cycle, and frontline nursing/tech staff) to surface workflow fit and adoption barriers; (3) a gap analysis against a readiness framework (data quality, security/compliance, workforce skill, decision governance); (4) a prioritized 12–18 month roadmap with staffing, phasing, and indicative cost bands; (5) a human-impact brief per use case naming which roles change, what they lose (e.g., radiologists losing first-read autonomy on triaged cases, transcriptionists' role folding into documentation review), what training is needed and its realistic duration, and who has override authority when the system and a clinician disagree.

**Out of scope:** model training or fine-tuning; EHR vendor contract negotiation; go-live support; measuring or guaranteeing clinical outcome improvement or cost savings — those depend on the network's own adoption and operational decisions post-roadmap.

**Conditional on client:** access to de-identified data samples for the 3 use cases (Owner: CIO/Data Governance lead); a named clinical sponsor per use case with authority to approve workflow change (Owner: CMO office); compliance sign-off to interview frontline staff and access system architecture diagrams (Owner: Compliance Officer); a standing 60-minute weekly steering session for 8 weeks (Owner: Program Sponsor).

**Deliverables & acceptance:**
- D1 Current-state assessment report — accepted when data owners confirm factual accuracy of infrastructure/governance findings.
- D2 Workforce/adoption brief per use case — accepted when named clinical sponsors sign off that role-impact statements are accurate and complete.
- D3 Prioritized roadmap with cost bands — accepted when steering committee approves scope and sequencing in writing.

Risks:
This engagement's timeline assumes clinical sponsors and compliance will grant interview and data access within week 1–2; regional hospital networks frequently need 4–6 weeks for IRB/compliance approval on staff interviews, which would compress or delay every downstream deliverable — flag this now rather than discover it in week 3. Radiologists, nurses, and transcriptionists interviewed may reasonably distrust a "readiness assessment" that precedes a headcount decision already made elsewhere; if that decision has been made, say so before we interview staff under a different premise. Data access limited to de-identified samples may understate real-world data quality issues (duplicate records, non-standard coding) that only appear at scale — the roadmap's cost bands will carry that uncertainty. Funding the assessment without pre-committing to change-management budget in the follow-on roadmap risks producing a technically sound plan that stalls in month four when frontline staff are asked to change workflows nobody funded them time to learn.
