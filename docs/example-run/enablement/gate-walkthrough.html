What to notice

The first approve is refused. Every artifact passed governance on its own content, but the Engagement Lead escalated three questions to the human, and an unanswered escalation flags the run. The system will not let a reviewer wave through work that the team itself said needs a person.

The override is permitted — sometimes the right call is to proceed and carry the question — but it costs a name and a written reason, and lands in the record as forced. Suppressing a concern is possible here and never silent.

annotate exists because a gate with only approve and reject teaches people to use neither. The reservation about unfunded backfill is recorded without blowing up the run, and it is still there when it turns out to matter.

reopen supersedes the approval without erasing it. The history reads approved → reopened → rejected, with who and why at every step, because a decision nobody can revisit is a decision people avoid making.
