Strategist Artifact

Read the full artifact 5,130 chars · sha256 f4248dacc… · revised

Objective

Sequence an AI transformation for a 400-person discrete manufacturer such that every phase's spend is gated on measured results from the phase before it, and no phase begins without a named human accountable for its go/no-go call.

Body

Phase 0 is a four-to-eight-week data and readiness audit, not a pilot. It establishes whether machine-level telemetry exists at sufficient coverage to support a predictive maintenance model at all, and it terminates the programme if it does not. The OT/security lead signs off before any live sensor connectivity, a gate added after review.

Phase 1 is a single-line 90-day pilot with maintenance technicians retaining override authority on every model-generated work order. Overrides are logged and reviewed weekly as information about the model, not as resistance to be managed.

Data Scientist Critique

Challenged by QA/QC · 3 findings · all accepted · artifact reissued

Steelman

The draft's core contribution is refusing to let the Strategist's roadmap be treated as measurable when it isn't — it forces a proxy versus outcome distinction, a hard data-quality gate before any modeling spend, a statistical floor before results can be claimed, and explicit labelling of the Deloitte benchmark as external rather than a client target.

Pre-mortem

Twelve months later the Phase 1 pilot clears its statistical floor and Phase 2 scales, but adoption stalls: technicians, seeing the model call false alarms, start quietly overriding or ignoring work orders because no one ever told them who has final say when their diagnosis disagrees with the model's.

1 ■■ Serious Human impact Accepted

Treating “override rate” as a leading indicator of technician distrust makes a metric do a control's job. The brief requires a named human checkpoint — who reviews overrides, on what evidence, with what authority, how recorded — before Phase 1 puts model-generated work orders in front of technicians.

Author

Added a named human checkpoint (who reviews overrides, on what evidence, with what authority, how recorded) before Phase 1 goes live, not just a metric.

2 ■■ Serious Human impact Accepted

The ROI formula nets out false-positive work orders times technician-hours as a cost, but is silent on whether the business case implicitly assumes reduced maintenance headcount once downtime-driven reactive labour falls. If that is the real trade-off, the house brief requires it stated plainly rather than left implicit in a savings formula.

Author

Added explicit language stating the ROI formula does not assume headcount reduction, and that if leadership intends one it must be named and modelled separately with its own human-impact analysis.

3 ■ Minor Consistency Accepted

Decision gates — Phase 0 coverage threshold, Phase 1 statistical floor, Phase 2 net-positive test — specify technical pass/fail conditions but not who is accountable for the go/no-go call when a gate is missed by a small margin.

Author

Named the accountable decision-maker for each gate, including who can authorize proceeding on a marginal miss and what they must document.
